# 07 — Harmonic function & cadences

**What to build:** The "why chords move" layer behind the function and cadence drills. A student can read what tonic/predominant/dominant function means and why progressions pull T→PD→D→T, then the full cadence taxonomy the curriculum drills: PAC vs IAC (the soprano criterion), half cadence, deceptive, plagal, the Phrygian half cadence (iv6–V), and the cadential 6/4 (why it is dominant-functioning despite its I6/4 spelling). Includes the SATB voice-leading vocabulary the voice-leading cadence labs assume: voices, common tones, tendency tones, resolution.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Triads, Roman numerals & inversions.

**Status:** ready-for-agent
