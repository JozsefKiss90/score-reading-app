7. Known limitations

- SATB is a deterministic nearest-soprano closed-position voicing, not a counterpoint engine (intentional; seam documented).
- Strict bass-first enforces full bottom-up order (the only mode the unchanged controller supports), and is restricted to inversion + block.
- Non-goals (Bach analysis, Schenkerian reduction, secondary dominants, 7th-chorddic minor) are unimplemented but each has a documented extension point.
