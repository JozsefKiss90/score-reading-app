# Dominant Seventh Chord

## Definition

The dominant seventh chord (V7) is the major triad on the fifth scale degree with a minor seventh stacked on top — in C major, G–B–D–F. The added seventh puts a [[Tritone]] (B–F) inside the chord, giving V7 its signature pull toward the tonic. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 09.)*
