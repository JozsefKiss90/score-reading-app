# Melodic Minor Scale

## Definition

The melodic minor scale raises both the sixth and seventh degrees of the [[Natural Minor Scale]] on the way up — smoothing the melodic climb into the tonic — and reverts to the natural form on the way down. In A minor, ascending: A B C D E F♯ G♯ A; descending: A G F E D C B A. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 08.)*
