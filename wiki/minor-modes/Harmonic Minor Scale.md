# Harmonic Minor Scale

## Definition

The harmonic minor scale is the [[Natural Minor Scale]] with its seventh degree raised a [[Semitone]], restoring a true [[Leading Tone]] and making the dominant triad major. In A minor: A B C D E F G♯ — the G♯ written as an [[Accidental]], while the [[Key Signature]] stays natural minor's. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 08.)*
