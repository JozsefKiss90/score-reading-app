# Cadential Six-Four

## Definition

The cadential 6/4 is the classic decoration of a cadence in which a second-inversion tonic *shape* (spelled I6/4) sounds over the dominant's bass note and settles onto V as the upper voices fall 6–5 and 4–3. Despite the tonic spelling, it behaves as an embellished dominant, not a point of rest — the one famous case where [[Chord Inversion]] changes what a chord *does*. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 07.)*
