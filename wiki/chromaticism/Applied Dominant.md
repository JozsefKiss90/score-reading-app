# Applied Dominant

## Definition

An applied (secondary) dominant is a dominant chord borrowed from another key to point at a chord other than the tonic — V of V, V of ii, and so on. It imports a [[Tritone]] from outside the key, aiming the same resolution pull at a temporary target. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 10.)*
