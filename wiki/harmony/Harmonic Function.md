# Harmonic Function

## Definition

Harmonic function is a chord's job in its [[Key]]: **tonic** chords are home and rest, **predominant** chords prepare tension, **dominant** chords carry it and demand resolution. Function groups the seven [[Diatonic Triads]] into three families that behave alike (vi can stand in for I, ii for IV), and it usually survives [[Chord Inversion]] — the chord keeps its job when its bass moves. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 07.)*
