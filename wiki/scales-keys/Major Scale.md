# Major Scale

## Definition

The major scale is a seven-note pattern of whole and half steps — W W H W W W H — starting from its tonic, so C major runs C D E F G A B. Every key on the circle of fifths is this one pattern starting from a different tonic. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 05.)*
