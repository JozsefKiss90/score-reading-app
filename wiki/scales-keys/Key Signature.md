# Key Signature

## Definition

A key signature is the group of sharps or flats printed just after the [[Clef]] at the start of every staff line. It applies its accidentals to every note of the matching letter, in every octave, for the whole piece — a key's standing spelling, so the music needn't mark an [[Accidental]] on each note individually. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 05.)*
