# Leading Tone

## Definition

The leading tone is the seventh [[Scale Degree]] when it sits a [[Semitone]] below the tonic — the note that *leads* upward into home. In C major it is B. The [[Major Scale]] has one built in; the [[Natural Minor Scale]] does not (its seventh degree, a whole tone below the tonic, is the *subtonic*), which is why minor-key harmony so often raises 7̂ — the story of the [[Harmonic Minor Scale]]. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 08.)*
