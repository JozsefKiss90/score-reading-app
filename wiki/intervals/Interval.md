# Interval

## Definition

An interval is the distance between two notes, named in two layers: a *number* from counting letter steps (a third, a fifth) and a *quality* from the exact size in semitones (major, minor, perfect). The [[Semitone]] and [[Whole Tone]] are the smallest intervals; stacking larger ones builds every chord. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 04.)*
