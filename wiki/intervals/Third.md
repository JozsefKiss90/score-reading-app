# Third

## Definition

A third is the interval spanning three staff positions — from a note to the note two scale steps above it. It comes in two diatonic sizes: the major third (four semitones) and the minor third (three semitones), and it is the building block chords are stacked from. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 04.)*
