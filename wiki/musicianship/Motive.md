# Motive

## Definition

A motive is a short melodic cell — a pattern of [[Scale Degree|scale degrees]] and rhythm — that keeps its identity when moved. 1̂–3̂–5̂–3̂ is the same motive in C major and E♭ major: [[Transposition]] changes every pitch and preserves the shape a listener recognises. *(Stub — expanded by ticket 11.)*
